# Ody Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 26, 2026**

Ody ("the App") is developed by Dan Zigmond. This privacy policy explains how the App collects, uses, and protects your information.

## Information We Collect

**Location Data.** The App uses your device's location to show nearby places and sort your saved places by distance. Location data is processed on your device and is not sent to any external servers.

**Name.** If you choose to connect with friends, the App stores the display name you provide so your friends can identify your shared places and notes.

**Places and Notes.** The App stores the places you save, along with any notes, tags, or other annotations you add. This data is synced via Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) to enable sharing with friends you connect with.

## How We Use Your Information

- To display your saved places and sort them by proximity
- To sync your places and notes across your devices via iCloud
- To share your places and notes with friends you explicitly connect with

## Data Storage and Sharing

All data is stored on your device and in your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service. We do not operate independent servers or databases.

When you connect with friends, the places and notes you have not marked as "Private" are shared with those friends through CloudKit. You control which places are private.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete any place or note at any time within the App. Deleting the App removes all local data. iCloud data can be managed through your device's iCloud settings.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
